title: CoA classes taken as a subclass get their class mechanics
---
As a subclass, the Wildwalker and Witch Hunter get their rage bar, and the Wildwalker can tame and call animal spirits and use Primal Weapons. The Demon Hunter gets its Fel Rifts, and the Reaper's Haunt works. Barbarian, Demon Hunter, Stormbringer, Sun Cleric, Tinker and Reaper talents that react to your hits work too.
