title: Ghost Wolf and Travel Form turn the whole ogre, and the twin brother rides the beast
---
Ghost Wolf, Wargstride and the druid's Travel Form change the whole body, also when a Two-Headed Ogre uses them from her subclass. The Thin Twins brother rides on the wolf's or cat's back. Server owners can turn this off with Subclass.TravelForms.WholeBody.
