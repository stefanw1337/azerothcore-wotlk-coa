title: Flying works again in Outland and Northrend, and no mount flies in Azeroth
---
Flying works with Expert Riding in Outland and Cold Weather Flying in Northrend. All mounts stay grounded elsewhere. Leaving a flying area dismounts the rider. Owners can restore the previous rules with CoA.LegacyMountFlight = 1.
