title: The Twins brother faces his target, leaps in and leaves a form to heal you
---
He no longer tries spells his current form forbids (the failed cast bar), takes a form again only after its cooldown, which for Accursed Form starts when the form ends, leaps or charges to reach a foe, and drops a form when you need a heal he can only cast without it.
