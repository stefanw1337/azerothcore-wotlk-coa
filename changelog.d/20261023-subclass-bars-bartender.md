title: Subclass Bar 1 works like an action bar and can live in Bartender4
---
Drag spells off it (hold Shift when your bars are locked), Alt-drag moves it, and Bartender4 gets a "Subclass Bar 1"
bar. Your own stance bar shows only your stances, also with DragonUI. /subclassbar reset brings every subclass bar back.
