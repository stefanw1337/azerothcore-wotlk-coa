title: Borrowed forms leave Two-Headed Ogres in their own body and turn the twin brother instead
---
A form or transformation from your subclass (Cat, Bear, Moonkin, Metamorphosis, Bloodmage forms) gives its effects but keeps your own look. For the Thin Twins your brother takes the form's shape and walks beside you while he has it; your own forms change only you, and he walks beside you then too.
