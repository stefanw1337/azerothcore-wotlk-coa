title: The Two-Headed Ogre's carried kart and mammoth bring their passengers along

When the Two-Players ogre body carries a D.R.I.V.E Kart or a Traveler's Tundra Mammoth, the kart's backseat merchants and the mammoth's vendors ride along in their seats on the carried mount.
