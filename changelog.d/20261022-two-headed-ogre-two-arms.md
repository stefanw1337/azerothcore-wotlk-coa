title: Two-Headed Ogres fight with a weapon in one hand and a gun in the other

With a hunter subclass, her main class keeps the weapon hand and casts with a fist at her heart, while the other head shoots the gun from her left hand. Raptor Strike and other melee strikes swing her mace, and she aims with mace on shoulder and gun in hand.
