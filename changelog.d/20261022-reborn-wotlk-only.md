title: Classic classes can be limited to their Wrath of the Lich King abilities
---
Set CoA.WarcraftReborn.WotlkOnly = 1 to remove the abilities Ascension added to the Warcraft Reborn classes, such as
Quick Shot and Shadow Artillery. They disappear from trainers and talent pages, and characters lose them at login.
