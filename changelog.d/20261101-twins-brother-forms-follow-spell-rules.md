title: The Thin Twins' brother follows a form's cost, cooldown and duration
---
Taking a form for the brother now pays its cost and starts its cooldown, and timed forms such as Accursed Form end when
their time runs out. Play easy, cowboy only picks lasting forms by itself. A brother at range no longer swings his
melee weapon there.
