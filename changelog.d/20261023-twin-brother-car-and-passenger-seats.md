title: The twin brother gets a real spot in cars and rides along in passenger seats
---
In cars, karts, trikes and similar mounts he sits on the rear deck or beside you instead of inside the seat back. On mounts with passenger seats, such as the Mekgineer's Chopper, he takes a free seat and gives it up as soon as another player climbs on.
