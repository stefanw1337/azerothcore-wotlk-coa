# Skinshifter - ideas for later

Parked until the basic class works. Nothing here is built.

## Monster play (Possessor)
Being the boss or a dangerous enemy, made fun for everyone: opt-in and with rewards for both sides.

- **Hunted** (open world, first to build): the Possessor takes over a rare/elite, which is marked
  ("Gorehowl (possessed by Estrella)") with a map marker. Hunters get a bounty (XP, gold, a unique reward); the
  Possessor earns rewards per minute survived and damage done. Ends on death or after 3-5 minutes. Only affects
  players who opted in (a Hunter flag or an event zone). Builds on True Possession.
- **The Possessed Gauntlet** (dungeon, optional): at a boss the party can let its Possessor play the boss, scaled to
  the group, with its own abilities plus a few mode-only boss abilities. Party wins: extra loot. Boss wins: a title
  and the boss's skin for the Possessor.
- **Night of the Possessed** (world event): a zone full of player-controlled monsters for an hour, with a
  leaderboard for both sides.
- Rules: never against players who did not opt in; no quest bosses or real raid bosses outside the Gauntlet; always
  a time limit and a clear way out; the monster is scaled so it is fair.

## True Possession (Possessor talent or capstone)
Take control of a mob (as `.possess`): the body vanishes into it, you come out where the mob is when you release;
the mob takes your level and health; 30-60 s, long cooldown; no elites, bosses or players. If the mob dies while
you are inside you are thrown out (short stun, some health lost). On release the mob is stunned briefly, then
hostile.

## Other
- PvP-only Possessor talent: take an enemy player's pet or totem for a few seconds (duels and battlegrounds).
- Possessor buffs: "Imposter Syndrome" (wearing a scholar/professor), "Skinshifting Genius!" (a real named
  professor), "Wolf in Sheep's Clothing" (sheep skin, next attack crits).
- Merchant's Pack at 20, Set Up Shop, Blacksmith repair (see Skinshifter-Design.md).
